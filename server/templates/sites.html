Calibrate a new site

You need photographs from the site's own camera, graded by an ophthalmologist or certified grader:

  • At least 20 referable and 20 non-referable photographs that pass the quality gate; 50 or more of each gives a usable estimate.
  • A CSV with an image column (file names) and a grade column (0–4) or a referable column (0/1).
  • Photographs refused by the quality gate are left out, as they would be in use.

Scoring takes a few seconds per photograph. For large batches use the command line: python -m fundalux.calibrate --site NAME --labels labels.csv --images DIR.
