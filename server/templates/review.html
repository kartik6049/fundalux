Clinician review

Grade photographs, blind

You will see fundus photographs one at a time, without Fundalux's answer. Grade each as you would in a screening programme. Your grades are compared with the system's and with the reference grades to show where it agrees with clinicians and where it does not.

How it works

  • {{ review["items"]|length }} photographs{% if review.name %} from “{{ review.name }}”{% endif %}. Most take 20–40 seconds; you can stop and continue later with the same name.
  • For each: is it gradable, the ICDR grade, whether you would refer for another reason (for example macular oedema), and how confident you are.
  • Each reviewer sees the photographs in their own random order.
  • Blinded (recommended) never shows Fundalux's answer. Teaching shows it after you commit your grade; those answers are marked and reported separately.
  • Your name is stored with your grades. No patient information is involved.

Keyboard: 0–4 grade · U ungradable · R refer for another reason · Z zoom · Enter save and next.
