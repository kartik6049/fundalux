Screening-camp model

What triage does to a camp

A discrete-event simulation of a screening camp: registration, photography with on-the-spot retakes, AI grading and remote ophthalmologist review of referrals. The benchmark camp sends every patient's photographs to the ophthalmologist. Timing, prevalence and cost values are placeholders to replace with local figures.
